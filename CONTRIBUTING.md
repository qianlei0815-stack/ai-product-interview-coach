# Contributing

Contributions are welcome.

1. Open an issue or describe the proposed change in a pull request.
2. Keep `SKILL.md` focused on the operating workflow; put expanded material in `reference.md`.
3. Add question seeds that require judgment rather than trivia.
4. Make scoring criteria observable and avoid unsupported claims about companies or candidates.
5. Keep examples usable without paid tools or private datasets.

By contributing, you agree that your work is available under the MIT License.
